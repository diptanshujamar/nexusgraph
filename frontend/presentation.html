The Indian Evidence Act is Repealed: Enter BSA 2023

How Section 63(4) of Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 transforms electronic evidence in courts

The Fatal Weakness in Cyber Prosecution

Under the previous Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872, electronic evidence was routinely dismissed by defense counsels citing lack of tamper-proof custody, custody gaps, and missing technical certifications.

Section 63(4) BSA 2023 Mandate:

Electronic records (CDRs, server logs, bank extracts, extracted chats) MUST be accompanied by a mandatory two-part certificate verifying hardware custody, system integrity, and cryptographic hash validation.

Failure to compute and seal evidence at the exact moment of ingestion renders the entire digital investigation inadmissible in High Courts and trial courts.

PHASE 2 GRAPH THEORY

Betweenness Centrality: Unmasking the Kingpin

Why degree count fails, and how broker influence reveals the real mastermind

Degree Centrality vs. Betweenness Centrality

Traditional police analysis focuses on call volume (Degree Centrality). But smart kingpins only make 1 or 2 high-level calls to their key lieutenants!

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION:
$$C_B(v) = \sum_{s \neq v \neq t} \frac{\sigma_{st}(v)}{\sigma_{st}}$$
Where $\sigma_{st}$ is total shortest paths between $s$ and $t$, and $\sigma_{st}(v)$ is paths passing through broker node $v$.
  • Broker Bottleneck: If node $v$ is removed, the entire syndicate's communication collapses.
  • Dynamic Sizing: In our D3 visualization, node radius scales dynamically with $C_B(v)$, making masterminds immediately prominent to investigators.
