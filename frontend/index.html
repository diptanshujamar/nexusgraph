Why Was Nexus Graph Built?

Criminal syndicates intentionally switch SIM cards, use slightly different name spellings across police stations (*"Rahul"* vs *"Raahul"*), move money across multiple mule bank accounts, and use getaway vehicles to evade detection.

Nexus Graph solves this by fusing unstructured First Information Reports (FIRs), Telecom Call Detail Records (CDRs), and Financial Logs into an automated Graph AI & Legal Cryptography Engine compliant with Section 63(4) of Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA 2023).
